Drone Surveys

Drone roof & site surveys, done properly.

We use professional drone equipment to inspect roofs, chimneys and hard-to-reach areas without putting anyone on a ladder. It means faster, safer, and far more accurate surveys — and you get to see exactly what we see.

When a drone survey helps

  • Spotting missing, cracked or slipped roof tiles before they cause a leak
  • Checking chimneys, flashing and valleys that are difficult or unsafe to reach
  • Assessing storm or wind damage for an insurance claim
  • Getting a clear look at a roof's condition before buying a property
  • Confirming the full scope of a roofing job before we quote, so there are no surprises

No scaffolding or ladders needed

A full external survey without the cost, time or risk of working at height just to take a look.

High-resolution photo & video reports

Every survey is delivered with clear imagery so you can see the exact condition of your roof, tiles and guttering.

Faster, more accurate quotes

Seeing the full roof from above means fewer surprises once work starts, and a quote you can trust.

Great for insurance & pre-purchase checks

Useful evidence for storm damage claims, subsidence concerns, or checking a property before you buy.

Fully licensed & insured

We hold CAA Operator and Flyer IDs, backed by full commercial drone insurance — every flight fully compliant.

From £259 +VAT

Available nationwide — any additional travel costs are simply agreed with you up front, based on location.
